UNIT 03 · LESSON 03

BCNF, multivalued dependencies & 4NF

IN PLAIN WORDS

BCNF checks every functional dependency: its left side must be a key. 4NF goes further and separates independent multi-valued facts.

By the end, you can:
  • Test a relation for BCNF
  • Explain multivalued dependency
  • Show why 4NF can remove repeated combinations
TOPICS IN THIS LESSON
01Boyce–Codd normal form (BCNF)↘02Multivalued dependency↘03Fourth normal form (4NF)↘
EXPLANATION
TOPIC 01

Boyce–Codd normal form (BCNF)

DEFINITION

A relation is in BCNF if, for every nontrivial functional dependency X → Y, X is a superkey.

A nontrivial dependency says the right side is not already part of the left side.

BCNF is stricter than 3NF. A BCNF decomposition is lossless when performed with the standard violating dependency, but it may not preserve every dependency locally.

QUICK EXAMPLE

If instructor → course is a rule but instructor is not a key in a teaching-assignment relation, that dependency violates BCNF.

TOPIC 02

Multivalued dependency

DEFINITION

X ↠ Y means that for one X value, the set of Y values is independent of the remaining attributes.

This is different from X → Y: there may be several Y values for one X.

If hobbies and languages are independent for each student, storing both in one table creates every hobby-language pairing.

QUICK EXAMPLE

A student with 2 hobbies and 3 languages can create 6 rows, even though the hobbies and languages are separate facts.

TOPIC 03

Fourth normal form (4NF)

DEFINITION

A relation is in 4NF when every nontrivial multivalued dependency has a superkey determinant.

4NF separates independent sets into different relations so unrelated values are not cross-multiplied.

After splitting, a join can recreate the possible combinations without storing each combination redundantly.

QUICK EXAMPLE

Separate StudentHobby(sid,hobby) and StudentLanguage(sid,language).

WORKED EXAMPLE · SELECT A STEP

Separate two independent lists

One student has two hobbies, chess and music, and speaks two languages, Tamil and Hindi. Neither list depends on the other.

HobbyLanguage
ChessTamil
ChessHindi
MusicTamil
MusicHindi
STEP 01

See why the combined table grows

The table pairs every hobby with every language. Two hobbies and two languages create four rows.

STEP 02

Notice the extra pairings

The four rows do not mean the student learned a language through a hobby. The lists are separate facts.

STEP 03

Store each list separately

Keep one table for Student-Hobby and another for Student-Language. This stores two hobby facts and two language facts.

RESULT

When two multi-value lists are independent, store them separately. This avoids storing every possible pair, which is the idea behind 4NF.

EXAM FOCUS

What to be ready to explain

Compare 3NF and BCNF. Define MVD and 4NF, then show a relation with independent multi-valued facts and its decomposition.
